01 / About

A short introduction

I am affiliated with Sichuan University (SCU). My current work explores how continuous state, sparse events, and persistent memory can work together in trainable sequence models.

This lightweight page is intentionally kept up to date. Details about current projects and collaborations are available by email.

02 / Research

What I study

A

Hybrid liquid-state sequence models

RG-LRU liquid reservoirs with learnable time constants and parallel recurrent scans for efficient long-context processing.

B

Event-driven associative memory

LIF spiking gates and Delta-rule memory for sparse, error-driven writing and retrieval.

C

Memory-augmented training systems

Inference-time resonance memory, carried state, truncated BPTT, and numerically precise GPU kernels.
